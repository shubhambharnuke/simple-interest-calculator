# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description
A simple interest calculator that calculates the interest earned or payable on a principal amount over a specified period at a given annual interest rate.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:
- **Principal:** Initial amount of money
- **Rate:** Annual interest rate (in percentage)
- **Time:** Time period in years

## Example

- Principal: 1000
- Rate: 5%
- Time: 2 years
- Simple Interest: 100
- Total Amount: 1100

## Technologies Used
- Python

## Usage
Enter the principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period to calculate simple interest.
